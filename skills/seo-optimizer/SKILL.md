# SEO optimizer
1. One primary query per page, plus 3-5 natural variants. Answer it in the first 100 words.
2. Title under 60 chars, meta under 155, one H1, descriptive H2s, short paragraphs, a real FAQ of questions people ask.
3. Entity clarity for AI engines: state what Pillar Fabric / Tenda is, who it is for, and one verifiable fact per claim; keep claims consistent across pages.
4. Internal links to live pages only. No keyword stuffing, no fabricated stats or reviews.
5. Output: revised text, title/meta, FAQ block, schema suggestion (Organization/FAQ) for the builder.

## House rules (all content skills)
- Product accounts are PILLAR FABRIC / Tenda. Product mailbox only. No invented founder backstory, seller quotes, metrics, or customers. Numbers only from verified sources.
- Story-first, founder-led: both twins front and centre where the story is theirs. Numbers are evidence, not the hook.
- Voice: direct, precise, with wit. Life-framed hooks, never corporate, never hype.
- Honest demo wording for Tenda: early demo, sample catalog, simulated MoMo, no live-payments claims, no waitlist CTA until capture is real.
- Personal LinkedIn/X posts under his name, and video uploads, wait for his yes. Nothing is "done" until reviewed end to end as a viewer.
