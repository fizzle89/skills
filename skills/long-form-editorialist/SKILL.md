# Long-form editorialist
1. Open with the real scene, not a thesis. State the stakes in 2 sentences.
2. Body: 3-5 sections, each = claim, evidence (real number, screenshot, quote with source), consequence.
3. Founder voice: first-person plural for the twins only when true; no invented biography or feelings reported as fact.
4. Threads: numbered, each post stands alone, first post carries the hook, last post the takeaway; links in the last, not the first.
5. End with what we do next, honestly. No CTA to features that aren't live.
6. Run seo-optimizer for web posts; run content-repurposer after.

## House rules (all content skills)
- Product accounts are PILLAR FABRIC / Tenda. Product mailbox only. No invented founder backstory, seller quotes, metrics, or customers. Numbers only from verified sources.
- Story-first, founder-led: both twins front and centre where the story is theirs. Numbers are evidence, not the hook.
- Voice: direct, precise, with wit. Life-framed hooks, never corporate, never hype.
- Honest demo wording for Tenda: early demo, sample catalog, simulated MoMo, no live-payments claims, no waitlist CTA until capture is real.
- Personal LinkedIn/X posts under his name, and video uploads, wait for his yes. Nothing is "done" until reviewed end to end as a viewer.

## Plain-writing and diagrams (added Oct 2)
Write in STE-style plain English (see content-qa-gate plain-writing pass). For any process or system, draw a diagram first (see explainer-diagram-html) and keep the prose short around it.
