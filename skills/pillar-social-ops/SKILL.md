# Pillar social operations

## Account structure (his rules)

- One real account per platform, product-branded Pillar Fabric, signups through the product mailbox - never mass accounts, never his Gmail.
- All stacks post: X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok. One set of accounts for the whole company; one YouTube channel covers every product.
- Accounts must actually post. An account that never posts is a failure state he calls out.
- Research any platform before posting on it: how content goes viral there right now, then implement the findings.
- Founders' personal profiles are separate founder surfaces; do not merge product and personal accounts.

## X (@usepillar_ai)

- Useful conversations and timely replies are editorial goals. The older ~150x weighting and first-60-minutes rule are unverified and must not be used as current ranking facts.
- 4-6 substantive replies per run on fresh posts from 2K-15K follower accounts; reply to every reply on own posts within the first hour.
- Take sides; confident one-sided opinions generate chains. No hedging. Hook in the first eight words.
- Use 1-2 relevant hashtags as an editorial default, not a verified ranking formula. Posts must stand alone without the click. Test native video against account analytics; no fixed 10-second boost is verified.
- Never pitch cold in DMs; warm DMs max 2/day to accounts already engaging.

## LinkedIn (company page + founders)

- 2-4 posts per week, never twice in 24 hours; stay on 2-4 defined themes for ~90 days before expecting distribution.
- Choose PDF, text or video for the proof being shown. Universal carousel superiority and a three-minute video threshold are not verified.
- A fixed 60% external-link penalty is not verified. Place the link where it serves the reader and test outcomes on the actual account.
- Company pages and founder profiles serve different audiences; do not assume a fixed reach cap.
- No engagement pods or comment automation; actively detected and penalized.

## Instagram / TikTok / YouTube

- Ghana seller content leads on Instagram/TikTok plus WhatsApp Status: 60-90s narrated walkthroughs with cutdowns.
- Every video: one CTA in caption and final frame (e.g. "DM AUDIT"). No empty teaser videos.
- Hashtags on posts (his explicit ask) - except X where 1-2 max.

## Execution notes

- Post through official APIs/connectors where available rather than browser automation; cold DMs stay manual from his phone because platform APIs do not allow business-initiated DMs.
- Track conversations, qualified replies, checkout starts and paid orders weekly. Decisions follow the funnel, not vanity views.
- The September 2026 playbook doc in his Drive ("Pillar Social Playbook") and the GTM plan doc hold the full platform research and offer-specific motions; consult them for depth. Posting itself needs the account access and authority in force at the time - this skill grants none.
