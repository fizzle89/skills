# Per-platform launch playbooks (researched Oct 3 2026)
Rule: copy FORMAT, structure, hook pattern, pacing. Never reuse others' media or text. $0 spend: skip any paid-creator tactic.

## LinkedIn (source: magicpost.in/blog/best-linkedin-post-format, 1.2M posts)
Median likes: image 34, carousel 34 (best creator reach 1,031), video 33 (only rising format), document 18, text 16, link 10, poll 6 (reach but no reward). So: carousel or one strong image per post; no polls, no link-in-post. First LinkedIn posts still go through him.
## X (source: flowjam.com/blog/launch-day-twitter-strategy-2026; others unread)
Weekday morning US Eastern beats weekend (claimed up to 15x). Founder hook tweet + native video; external video links are under-distributed; reply to every reply in the first hour; behind-the-scenes sub-thread for bookmarks; an idle account gets ~50 impressions so warm up first. Skip the paid creator block. @usepillar_ai is paused (label), nothing to apply yet.
## TikTok (source: slidetik.ai/blog/how-to-make-viral-tiktok-slideshows-for-saas)
SaaS slideshow: 9 slides, under 30s, one claim hook in 1.8s, big high-contrast text, no logo on slide 1, slides 2-7 one idea each, slide 8 proof (real screenshot), slide 9 one action. Completion rate is the metric. Faceless works.
## YouTube Shorts (source: aibrify.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-curve-playbook)
Cliff curve (30-50% drop in 1-3s) kills reach. Open on the most visually loaded frame, no intro card, no logo animation, no "hey guys". Aim ~80% average viewed; loop-friendly endings.

## Observed live (yt-dlp metadata only, Oct 3): short animated SaaS demo clips of 20-50s pull 200k-490k views; long how-to videos of 13-16 min pull far less. Pattern: one product moment, motion, under 50s.
