# Content ideation engine
## Inputs
Voice sheet, real assets (screens, recordings, shipped changes, verified numbers), calendar so far, platform mechanics notes in pillar-social-ops.
## Method
1. List true raw material only: what shipped, what broke, what was learned, a number we can show.
2. For each, write the idea as: tension (what a viewer already feels) -> real moment -> evidence -> one takeaway.
3. Tag pillar: story / proof / how-it-works / opinion / behind-the-build.
4. Score 1-5: truth-checkable, has an asset today, platform fit, uniqueness. Drop anything under 12/20.
5. Output 5-10 ideas with chosen platform and format. Hand the top one to hook-architect.
## Reject
Invented stories, fake testimonials, generic tips, anything needing an asset we lack (log it as a gap instead).

## House rules (all content skills)
- Product accounts are PILLAR FABRIC / Tenda. Product mailbox only. No invented founder backstory, seller quotes, metrics, or customers. Numbers only from verified sources.
- Story-first, founder-led: both twins front and centre where the story is theirs. Numbers are evidence, not the hook.
- Voice: direct, precise, with wit. Life-framed hooks, never corporate, never hype.
- Honest demo wording for Tenda: early demo, sample catalog, simulated MoMo, no live-payments claims, no waitlist CTA until capture is real.
- Personal LinkedIn/X posts under his name, and video uploads, wait for his yes. Nothing is "done" until reviewed end to end as a viewer.
