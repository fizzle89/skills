# Hook architect
1. Produce 10 hooks for the idea across patterns: concrete number, contradiction, specific moment, plain question, before/after, "what I got wrong".
2. Per platform: X first line stands alone and is under ~200 chars; LinkedIn first 2 lines before "see more"; Reels/TikTok/Shorts on-screen text plus first spoken line inside 2 seconds; carousel slide 1 readable at thumbnail size.
3. Cut any hook that overpromises, is vague, or can't be proven by the body. No clickbait the content doesn't pay off.
4. Rank top 3 with one-line reason; pick one plus a fallback.
5. Check against voice sheet. Pass the pick to short-form-scriptwriter, carousel-builder, or long-form-editorialist.

## House rules (all content skills)
- Product accounts are PILLAR FABRIC / Tenda. Product mailbox only. No invented founder backstory, seller quotes, metrics, or customers. Numbers only from verified sources.
- Story-first, founder-led: both twins front and centre where the story is theirs. Numbers are evidence, not the hook.
- Voice: direct, precise, with wit. Life-framed hooks, never corporate, never hype.
- Honest demo wording for Tenda: early demo, sample catalog, simulated MoMo, no live-payments claims, no waitlist CTA until capture is real.
- Personal LinkedIn/X posts under his name, and video uploads, wait for his yes. Nothing is "done" until reviewed end to end as a viewer.

## Lessons from first keyed run (Oct 2, Groq gpt-oss-120b)
- No numeric hook (percent, hours, counts) unless the number is in a cited source file or visible in the product. Invented stats like "90% of DM sellers" are an automatic cut.
- No first-person founder confessions ("I thought I could handle it solo", "I built my catalog but...") unless the founders supplied that exact story. Never fabricate a founder anecdote.
- "Before/After" and "zero X" claims are outcome claims the early demo cannot back. Use "one inbox, four sample roles" style facts only.
- Never justify a hook with a made-up source ("survey of early users").
- Output ranking reasons must reference only the input text.
