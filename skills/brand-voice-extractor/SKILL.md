# Brand voice extractor
Chain: **this** -> content-ideation-engine -> hook-architect -> format skill -> packaging -> content-repurposer.

## Steps
1. Collect 10-20 real samples: his voice notes (observations phone_messages), live posts, approved drafts, playbook doc. Never use scraped competitor copy as voice.
2. Extract: sentence length range, openers he uses, words he avoids, humour type, how he states numbers, how twins/"we" appear.
3. Write a voice sheet (max 15 lines): Do / Don't / 5 sample lines / banned words (hype, "revolutionary", "game-changer", corporate filler, emdashes).
4. Mark gaps ("no sample for X") instead of inventing a backstory or personality facts.
5. Save to /downloads/voice-<brand>.md and reuse it as input to every later skill.

## House rules (all content skills)
- Product accounts are PILLAR FABRIC / Tenda. Product mailbox only. No invented founder backstory, seller quotes, metrics, or customers. Numbers only from verified sources.
- Story-first, founder-led: both twins front and centre where the story is theirs. Numbers are evidence, not the hook.
- Voice: direct, precise, with wit. Life-framed hooks, never corporate, never hype.
- Honest demo wording for Tenda: early demo, sample catalog, simulated MoMo, no live-payments claims, no waitlist CTA until capture is real.
- Personal LinkedIn/X posts under his name, and video uploads, wait for his yes. Nothing is "done" until reviewed end to end as a viewer.
